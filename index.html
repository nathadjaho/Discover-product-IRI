Confidentiality and terms

Version 1.1 · 8 October 2026

  1. What you share

    What you share during the session stays confidential. IRI uses it only to design its product. It is never published, sold or passed on to anyone else.

  2. What we show

    Mock-ups, demos and plans shown during the session are confidential. Please do not share, copy or capture them, or use them for a competing project.

  3. Other participants

    If other people attend the same session, what they say is covered by the same confidentiality.

  4. Recording

    The session is recorded only if you agree below. Otherwise only written notes are taken. Any recording is deleted within 90 days.

  5. Anonymous summaries

    Your answers may be grouped with others in internal summaries, without your name or your company's. Nothing is quoted under your name without your written consent.

  6. Your own customers' data

    Please do not share personal data about your customers or business secrets you are not allowed to disclose. An anonymised example is always enough.

  7. Free participation

    Taking part creates no obligation to buy or partner. You can cancel or leave at any time, and ask us to delete your details and answers.

  8. Duration

    Terms 1 to 3 apply for two years from the session date. Information that is already public is not covered.
